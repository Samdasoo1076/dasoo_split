Why the switch of a 2 u+ key is turned 90 degrees
  With the switch in line, hot swap socket pad 2 (x 7.225 .. 9.775, y
  -5.05 .. -2.55 from the key centre) runs 0.875 x 1.2 mm into the right hand
  cutout (x 8.9 .. 14.9, y -3.75 .. 3.75) - the pad would be milled away - and
  the socket body would sit where the stabilizer housing protrudes below the
  PCB (the same interference Salicylic-acid3 warns about: "turn the switch
  sideways").  Turned 90 degrees the socket pads stay inside x -7.3 .. +7.3,
  which clears both cutouts by more than 0.2 mm.
  Because pad 2 then sits at cx + 3.8, cy + 8.5, that key's diode moves from
  x 6.75 to 7.68 mm and the switch-to-diode stub drops below the diode
  cathode (y = cy + 10.35) before entering the anode pad, keeping 0.2 mm to
  the cathode pad and 0.925 mm to the row via.

Row bus
  The F.Cu row bus steps 45 degrees above each 3.0 x 4.0 clip hole of a wide
  key (0.5 mm from the hole edge, 0.375 mm of copper clearance) and the maze
  router treats all 20 oval holes and all 10 cutouts as keepouts on both
  layers.
